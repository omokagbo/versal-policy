# Versal Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 20, 2026
**Developer:** Emmanuel Omokagbo
**Contact:** emmanuel.omokagbo@gmail.com

Versal is a Bible verse app. You pick how you feel, and it suggests verses. This policy explains what Versal stores, what it sends off your device, and what you can do about it.

## The short version

- Your verses, collections, mood check-ins, journal entries and settings are stored on your device and in your own iCloud. I cannot see them.
- Versal has no accounts, no ads and no advertising tracking.
- Versal uses three third-party services for app diagnostics and session recordings. They are described in section 3.

## 1. What stays on your device and in your iCloud

Versal stores the following:

- Favorite verses, collections and their order.
- Mood check-ins, including the mood, the intensity and any text you typed.
- Journal entries you write about verses.
- Reading sessions, check-in history and streak data.
- Reminder and appearance settings.

This data is saved on your device. If iCloud is on, it syncs through **your private iCloud database** using Apple's CloudKit. Apple's iCloud privacy terms govern that sync. I do not have access to your iCloud database, and I do not run a server that receives your content.

The bundled Bible text, including its search index, is read-only and stored on your device.

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Versal does not use a push server for them.

## 2. What you control

- **Export.** Settings → Your data → Export creates a file with your journal, favorites and collections. You choose where the file goes.
- **Delete.** Settings → Your data → Delete all removes Versal's data from your device and from your private iCloud.
- **Notifications.** You can turn reminders off in Settings or in iOS Settings.
- **Sharing.** When you share a verse, Versal passes it to the iOS share sheet. What happens next depends on the app you share to.

## 3. Third-party services

Versal uses the following third-party services. Each one receives only the data described here.

### Firebase (Google)

Versal uses Firebase for three things:

- **Crashlytics.** When the app crashes, it sends a crash report. The report includes technical details such as the device model, the operating system version, the app version and the time of the crash, plus an installation identifier that Firebase generates.
- **Performance Monitoring.** It measures app performance, such as startup time and network timing, and sends those measurements to Firebase.
- **Remote Config.** It fetches a small set of configuration values from Firebase. Firebase needs an identifier to deliver them.

Firebase Analytics is turned off, so Versal does not send usage events to Google for analytics.

Google's handling of this data is described in the [Firebase privacy information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

### Microsoft Clarity

Versal uses Microsoft Clarity to record how screens are used. A session recording captures taps, scrolling, navigation and screen layout, along with basic device and app information, so that I can see where the app gets confusing.

Journal entries and check-in notes are hidden from these recordings. Verse text is not personal and is not hidden.

Clarity keeps session recordings for 90 days.

Clarity's handling of this data is described in the [Microsoft Clarity privacy statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement).

## 4. Sensitive content

Mood check-ins and journal entries can reveal how you are doing. If you write about self-harm or crisis, Versal may show a card with crisis resources. Versal is not a substitute for professional help. If you are in immediate danger, contact your local emergency number.

## 5. Children

Versal is not intended for anyone under 14. I do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 14. If you believe someone under 14 has used Versal, contact me at **emmanuel.omokagbo@gmail.com** and I will help.

## 6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I will update the effective date at the top and publish the new version at the same address. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

## 7. Contact

For questions about this policy or your data, email **emmanuel.omokagbo@gmail.com**.
